About Me

Design is how I
found my voice

Hi, I'm Naqiya — a designer and final-year student at B.S. Abdur Rahman Crescent Institute of Science & Technology, Chennai. My design work has grown entirely through university — from academic projects to leading the visual identity of the Crescent Literary Society as its President.

I work across editorial and social media graphics, print and event collateral, and 3D geospatial visualisation — an unusual combination that reflects both my design instincts and my academic background. Every project here is something I made, for a real context, with real intent behind it.
